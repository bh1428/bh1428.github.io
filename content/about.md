This is the personal website of Ben Hattem. I am a Technology Consultant, Python developer and Full Stack Troubleshooter living in [The Netherlands][the_netherlands]. In my spare time I like to tinker with [Raspberry Pi's][raspberry_pi] and play games like [Factorio][factorio] and [Eve Online][eve_online].

This site is a static website created using [Hugo][hugo] with a [template][template_repo] based on the [m10c][m10c_repo] template by [vaga][vaga]. It is hosted on [GitHub Pages][github_pages] and deployed through a [Github workflow][github_workflows] from a [Github repository][site_repo]. All this may (or may not) change in the future.

I want to clarify that everything on this site reflects my personal opinions, which may be right or wrong (you decide). The only certainty I have is that I don't know everything. That may be unfortunate, but it is what it is.

In case you want, you can reach me via benghattem (at) gmail (dot) com.
